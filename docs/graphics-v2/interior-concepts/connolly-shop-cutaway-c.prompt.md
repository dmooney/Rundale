Primary request: Connolly's Shop interior concept C for Rundale. Resolve
gameplay-fit concerns from previous Connolly A/B candidates while preserving

NPC/game requirements: Roisin Connolly, 38, shopkeeper, lives and works here.
Her biography says she runs the shop with her mother. Her schedule says she

meal/flour/oats; baskets/crates for eggs and butter; candles/twine/ironmongery;

Style/medium: handmade illustrated parish notebook watercolor with ink
outlines, warm muted palette, rough lived-in rural materials, modest early shop,
not grand Victorian general store, not American frontier fantasy, not polished

Lighting/mood: neutral daylight from small window/doorway plus soft hearth or
candle warmth, clear enough for gameplay hotspots.

Avoid: no people, no animals, no UI, no readable text or brand labels, no cash
register, no glass display case, no modern packaging, no electric lighting, no

stair/ladder/hatch, curtain, or clear threshold.
