## A: Rich D-Style Pawns on 3x Map

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Graphics V2 overhead gameplay character-scale concept
Input images: Image 1 is the D sprite/pawn sheet; use it for character treatment, rural Irish clothing, watercolor pawn readability, and selection halo ideas. Image 2 is the 3x overhead map-scale mockup; use it for map scale, camera height, road/building/garden proportions, and how larger tokens sit on the enlarged surface.

Primary request: Create a focused gameplay mockup combining D-style overhead player/NPC pawns with the 3x overhead watercolor map surface.

Scene/backdrop: a 3x zoomed overhead watercolor map section with pale dirt road, yard, flat roof-footprint buildings, garden/orchard rows, hedges/banks, trees, and fields. Keep it an overhead map surface, not an isometric scene. The map should be large enough that D-sized pawns feel plausible and readable.

Subject: 8-10 D-style hand-painted top-down pawns placed naturally on the map: player traveler on the lane, shawled woman near a building, farmer by the garden, older laborer at field edge, child near yard, dark-coated priest/schoolmaster/shopkeeper type, two villagers in conversation, and one distant walker. Use the D sheet's top-down hat/shawl/cloak/body treatment, but slightly simplify details so they read at gameplay scale.

Style/medium: black-brown ink and transparent watercolor; muted 1820 rural Irish clothing colors: brown wool, grey, ochre, dark green, faded blue, off-white linen, black coat. Characters have tiny hats, shawls, coats, aprons, and packs visible from overhead, with no readable facial detail.

Gameplay treatment: one selected player has a faint warm parchment wash halo; one talkable NPC has a very small muted accent mark. Interaction marks must be subtle and hand-drawn, like ink/watercolor marks on the map.

Composition/framing: one full-frame overhead gameplay concept, no labels, no UI panel, no readable text, no captions. Show enough road, yard, garden, building edge, and field boundary to judge scale.

Constraints: characters should be about D-sheet pawn size relative to the 3x map, readable but not giant. Keep buildings mostly flat as map roof footprints; avoid turning the scene into perspective architecture. Keep all people overhead/top-down.

Avoid: side-view figures, portraits, chibi heads, 3D miniatures, modern map pins, neon UI, speech bubbles, nameplates, health bars, readable text, fantasy clothing, weapons, animals, carts, smoke, strong cast shadows, isometric camera.
```

## B: D-Style Pawns on Flatter 3x Map

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Graphics V2 overhead gameplay character-scale concept
Input images: Image 1 is the D sprite/pawn sheet; use it for readable hand-painted player/NPC pawns and period clothing. Image 2 is the 3x map-scale mockup; use it for enlarged gameplay scale only, but flatten the buildings and boundaries more than Image 2.

Primary request: Create a cleaner 3x overhead map gameplay mockup with D-style readable pawns, but keep the environment flatter and more cartographic than the previous 3x map.

Scene/backdrop: 3x zoomed flat overhead watercolor map surface: pale dirt road, yard, flat dark roof-footprint shapes, garden/orchard rows, hedges/banks, trees, fields. Buildings should read as flat map roof footprints with ink hatching, not perspective buildings with visible walls. Roads and yards are flat pale ribbons/spaces. Hedges and boundaries are thin map marks, not tall walls.

Subject: 7-9 D-style top-down pawns integrated into the map at readable scale: player traveler with hat on the road, shawled woman near a roof footprint, farmer at garden edge, older laborer near field, child near yard, dark-coated priest/schoolmaster/shopkeeper type, two villagers in conversation. Pawns can be slightly simplified but should keep D's warmth, clothing, and human readability.

Style/medium: delicate black-brown ink and transparent watercolor. Muted historical rural palette: browns, grey wool, ochre, dark green, faded blue, off-white linen, black coat. Small soft grounding washes only, no strong shadows.

Gameplay treatment: subtle selected-player halo only; optional tiny warm brush accent on one talkable NPC. These marks must look hand-painted, not modern UI.

Composition/framing: full-frame overhead gameplay sample, no labels, no text, no captions, no UI panel. Show road width, yard space, roof footprints, garden rows, hedges, and characters clearly enough to judge scale.

Constraints: map is the main gameplay surface; characters are readable but secondary. Keep all people top-down. Keep camera strictly overhead and flat.

Avoid: side-view people, large portraits, modern pins, neon UI, speech bubbles, nameplates, health bars, 3D miniatures, strong cast shadows, perspective/isometric buildings, visible doors/windows/walls, fantasy costumes, animals, carts, smoke, readable text, compass, border.
```
