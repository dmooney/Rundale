## Quick Read

This direction is viable: D-sized pawns fit a `3x` overhead map surface much
better than the full-map scale. The next refinement should keep the Cycle CD B
map discipline while recovering a little more of Cycle CD A's character warmth.

Prompt pressure to keep for the next pass:

- D-style hand-painted top-down pawns;
- readable hats, shawls, coats, aprons, and packs;
- very subtle selected-player halo only;
- 3x overhead map scale;
- flat roof-footprint buildings, not perspective architecture;
- no labels, nameplates, speech bubbles, health bars, or modern map pins.

## Recommended Next Prompt

Use `idea-cd-b-d-pawns-flat-map-3x.png` as the gameplay-base reference and
`idea-cd-a-d-pawns-rich-3x-map.png` only for warmer pawn clothing and human
readability. Generate a cleaner 3x overhead watercolor gameplay surface with
D-style top-down pawns, flat roof-footprint buildings, pale walkable lanes and
yards, garden/orchard marks, irregular hedges/banks, and subtle hand-painted
selection marks. Keep the camera strictly overhead and cartographic. Avoid
perspective buildings, side-view people, modern map pins, labels, speech
bubbles, health bars, strong shadows, animals, carts, smoke, and readable text.
