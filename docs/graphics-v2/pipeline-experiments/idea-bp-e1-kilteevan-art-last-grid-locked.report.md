# BP E1 Art-Last Grid-Locked Report

## Verdict

Pass as geometry/order evidence. E1 shows that reversing the BO order works
better than asking the same pass to both rectify perspective and recover style:
start from the perspective-first/rectified BO E2 plate, add a hard isomorphic
grid as a projection law, then repaint the art last.

## What Worked

- The final image does not show the guide grid.
- Roads, roof ridges, garden rows, and wall/garden edges mostly stay in
  parallel low-oblique families.
- The BO fish-eye/barrel feel is reduced without returning to a high survey
  board.
- Doors remain readable on the visible walkable facades.
- The art is closer to the original notebook than BO E2: more ink texture,
  mottled vegetation, and stronger material read.

## Caveats

- The palette is still too sepia and even compared with the original notebook.
- Garden rows and enclosure edges remain too diagrammatic.
- The render has a clean generated finish, especially in roof grids and garden
  blocks.

## Use

Treat E1 as the stricter geometry baseline for Cycle BP. It is useful proof
that the hard grid can steer the final projection without becoming visible, but
it is not yet the preferred style target.
