## Audit

- Door height: improved versus BR E1. The main doors are materially smaller
  and sit closer to the concept-art shop/church/cottage door scale. They still
  read clearly at plate scale.
- Door presence: the visible walkable-facade openings in the main compound read
  as fitted plank doors with thresholds/steps. I do not see an obvious
  person-sized black void in the main compound.
- Style: still close to the notebook target: loose ink, warm paper, watercolor
  thatch, muddy road, mottled walls, and dense hand-painted garden/vegetation
  detail.
- Camera: zoomed out enough to reduce the close-up door scale, but still a
  local playable plate rather than a wide survey board.

## Caveats

- The garden and perimeter walls are cleaner and more physical than the loose
  original notebook sample. This pass should not be used as evidence that wall
  and garden semantics are solved.
- The result is an edit from BR E1, so it is visual-target evidence, not
  direct-map recipe evidence.
- The original BR close control contains dark openings; future prompts should
  continue treating door-fixed crops and the "doors on openings" rule as higher
  authority than those control shadows.

## Use

Use BS E1 as the concept-art branch's current door-height target: zoom out
enough that doors match the original notebook scene, then enforce fitted plank
doors on every visible walkable-facade opening.
