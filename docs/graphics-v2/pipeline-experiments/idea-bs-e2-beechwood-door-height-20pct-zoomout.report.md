# BS E2 Beechwood 20% Zoom-Out Report

## Verdict

Pass for the requested bounded adjustment: E2 zooms out roughly another 20%
from BS E1 while keeping the connected Beechwood compound, fitted plank doors,
and the warm notebook-style rendering family.

It is not an outright style win over BS E1. The wider view improves scale but
also makes the plate feel a little more clean, planned, and estate-map-like
than the original concept art.

## Comparison Notes

- Scale: E2 is visibly farther out than BS E1. The compound occupies less of
  the frame and there is more surrounding road, tree, garden, and boundary
  context.
- Door height: the main doors remain readable and are closer to the original
  concept-art game-scene size than BR E1's oversized close-up doors.
- Door presence: visible walkable-facade openings in the main compound still
  read as fitted doors rather than dark empty voids.
- Style: E2 keeps the thatch, warm paper, muddy road, and watercolor texture,
  but it is cleaner and more orderly than the concept art. Garden rows and
  walls remain too regular compared with the original notebook scene.
- Camera: the wider view is useful, but it also raises the perceived camera
  slightly and weakens the close, lived-in facade presence of BS E1.

## Use

Use E2 when the priority is a slightly wider playable frame and concept-art
door scale. Use BS E1 when the priority is richer close concept-art presence.
Neither should be treated as direct recipe proof; both are visual targets in
the relaxed concept-art branch.
