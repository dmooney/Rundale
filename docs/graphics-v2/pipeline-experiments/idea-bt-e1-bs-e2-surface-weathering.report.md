# BT E1 Surface Weathering Report

## Verdict

Useful but too conservative. E1 makes BS E2 dirtier and more textured without
breaking the compound, doors, crop, or road/garden relationships. It reduces
the pristine finish, but the plate still reads as an orderly estate-board
environment rather than the original concept art's messy lived-in village
realism.

## What Worked

- Preserved BS E2's 20% zoomed-out frame and connected compound.
- Kept doors readable as fitted plank doors.
- Added stronger mud, lichen, wall staining, thatch variation, and darker road
  wear.
- Did not add obvious new structures, signage, people, animals, smoke, or
  landmark leakage.

## Weaknesses

- Too little incidental life. The yard is still sparse and controlled.
- Garden rows and perimeter walls remain regular and planned.
- The concept-art gap remains: this is weathered, but not convincingly used.
