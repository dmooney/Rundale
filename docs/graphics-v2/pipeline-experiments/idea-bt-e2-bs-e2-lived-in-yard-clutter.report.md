# BT E2 Lived-In Yard Clutter Report

## Verdict

Best single tested direction for concept realism. E2 keeps BS E2's scale and
compound while adding the kind of small rural clutter, threshold wear, and
yard-use marks that make the place feel inhabited rather than pristine.

## What Worked

- Added small buckets/barrels/crates/tools in plausible places near doors,
  walls, and the yard.
- Strengthened muddy road and threshold wear.
- Preserved the connected compound, road, garden, and fitted doors.
- Moved closer to the original concept art's "used place" feeling without
  adding people, animals, labels, smoke, or new buildings.

## Weaknesses

- Some bucket/barrel repetition appears, especially along the walls and garden
  edge. The final prompt should cap repeated round containers.
- Garden rows and walls are still more regular than the concept art.
- The clutter helps, but the geometry still needs a little handmade
  irregularity.
