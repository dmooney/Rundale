# BU E1 Concept-Realism Hybrid Report

## Verdict

Good hybrid pass, but not the stopping point. BU E1 successfully combines BT
E2's lived-in yard direction with some of BT E3's handmade irregularity while
keeping the BS E2 scale, connected compound, and fitted-door discipline.

It still carries a little too much repeated small-object clutter, especially
round bucket/container shapes, and some wall/garden structure remains more
regular than the original illustrated parish notebook.

## What Worked

- Preserved the connected Beechwood compound, road, yard, garden, and tree
  masses from the BS/BT branch.
- Kept visible fitted plank doors instead of returning to black doorway voids.
- Warmed and roughed the watercolor/ink surface without making the whole plate
  as dark and heavy as BT E3.
- Added more believable muddy wear, threshold scuffing, moss, weeds, and
  locally broken road/garden edges.

## Weaknesses

- Repeated round container shapes still read as a pattern in a few areas.
- Garden and wall geometry is improved but still a little estate-plan-like.
- It moves closer to the concept-art material language, but needs one more
  tightening pass focused on removing repeated prop motifs and softening the
  remaining regularity.
