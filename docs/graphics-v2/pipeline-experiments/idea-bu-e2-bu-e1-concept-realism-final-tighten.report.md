# BU E2 Concept-Realism Final Tighten Report

## Verdict

Accepted convergence point for the relaxed concept-art branch. BU E2 is the
closest match so far to the original illustrated parish notebook's environment
realism while preserving the BS E2 zoom target, connected Beechwood compound,
readable fitted doors, no-UI background-plate constraint, and broad road/garden
layout.

I would stop here for this branch. Further prompt-only polish is more likely to
over-darken the plate, reintroduce repeated props, obscure doors, or spend
topology budget than to produce a meaningful concept-art gain.

## What Worked

- Keeps every obvious walkable-facade opening as a fitted plank door with a
  threshold, rather than a black void.
- Reduces BU E1's repeated bucket/barrel pattern while retaining a few practical
  rural details.
- Preserves the compound, yard, road, garden, walls, and trees closely enough
  for this visual-target branch.
- The value range is warmer and closer to the concept art than BT E3: dirty and
  worn, but still readable.
- Walls, limewash, thatch, road edges, weeds, and garden rows now feel more
  handmade and lived-in than BS E2/BT E2.

## Remaining Gap

- The original concept art is a busier village crossroads with people, UI
  labels, a shop/church context, and more varied civic clutter. BU E2 is a
  quieter no-UI Beechwood compound, so it cannot match that subject density
  without changing the source/location constraints.
- The garden remains slightly more organized than the concept art's most
  irregular areas, but loosening it further would likely reduce map readability.
- This is visual-target/edit evidence, not a fresh one-shot map-to-art recipe,
  because it refines prior rendered plates.
