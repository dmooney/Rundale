# BV E1 Grove Reproducible BU-Style Report

## Verdict

Direct pipeline pass. BV E1 shows the BU concept-realism target can transfer to
Grove without using a previous Grove render as an edit target.

The output preserves Grove's separate-building yard topology, road exits,
garden block, gates, walls, trees, and readable fitted doors. It does not copy
Beechwood's connected compound layout. The main weakness is that it still feels
a little clean/high compared with BU E2.

## What Worked

- Grove remains a separate-building yard instead of becoming a connected
  Beechwood-style compound.
- The lower long building range, left/west small building, central yard
  building, taller east/right building, road curve, garden block, and gates all
  remain legible.
- Obvious walkable facades have fitted plank doors and thresholds.
- No UI, labels, people, animals, water, bridge, church, graveyard, shop, smoke,
  or extra scenic landmark appears.
- BU E2's warm worn palette, ink texture, muddy roads, and handmade vegetation
  transfer better than prior clean Grove controls.

## Weaknesses

- Still a little higher, cleaner, and more control-plate-like than BU E2.
- Garden rows remain organized and wall edges remain somewhat regular.
- The direct render is a recipe proof, but the visual target benefits from one
  bounded style/scale correction.
