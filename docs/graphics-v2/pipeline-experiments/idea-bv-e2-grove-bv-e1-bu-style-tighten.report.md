# BV E2 Grove BU-Style Tighten Report

## Verdict

Preferred Grove result for Cycle BV. BV E2 is the single bounded correction from
BV E1 and is the current best evidence that the BU-style pipeline can produce a
usable final plate for another location.

It keeps Grove's separate buildings, doors, roads, yard, gates, and garden
relationships while moving the surface finish closer to BU E2: warmer, rougher,
more worn, and less like a clean control plate.

## What Worked

- Preserved Grove-specific topology rather than copying Beechwood's connected
  compound.
- Kept fitted plank doors and thresholds visible on the walkable facades.
- Improved limewash staining, roof/weathering strokes, muddy road scumble,
  weeds, stone-wall texture, and handmade garden variation.
- Kept the scene no-UI and free of labels, people, animals, church, bridge,
  water, smoke, shop signs, and extra scenic landmarks.
- Kept clutter sparse; no repeated bucket/barrel/crate pattern is visible.

## Caveats

- A tiny roof-nub/vent-like mark appears on the taller east/right building. It
  should be watched or fixed if this plate is promoted further.
- The garden remains somewhat regular compared with the original concept art,
  but it is more source-readable than a fully loosened repaint would be.
- This is a bounded correction from BV E1. Treat BV E1 as the direct recipe
  evidence and BV E2 as the preferred visual output.
