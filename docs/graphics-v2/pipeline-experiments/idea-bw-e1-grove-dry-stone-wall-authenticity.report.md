## Result

E1 is a partial pass and a material-audit failure.

It preserves the Grove scene well: the separate yard buildings, road curve,
garden block, gates, open yard, and fitted plank doors remain readable. The
image also keeps the BU/BV warm worn watercolor finish.

The targeted dry-stone correction is not strong enough. The walls are less like
cut rectangular masonry than BV E2, but many runs still read as repeated
same-sized rounded stones, especially along the foreground and garden edges.
That creates a bead-chain / cobblestone-top-row effect rather than authentic
historic Irish fieldstone.

## Audit

- Grove topology: pass.
- Doors/thresholds: pass.
- No new props/UI/water/church/graveyard: pass.
- Wall authenticity: fail; too many repeated round stones and continuous tidy

- Usefulness: keep as evidence that the dry-stone direction is safe for Grove
  topology, but do not accept it as the wall-material target.
