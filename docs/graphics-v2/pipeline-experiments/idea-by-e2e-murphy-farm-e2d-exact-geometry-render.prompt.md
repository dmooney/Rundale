# Idea BY E2e — Murphy's Farm E2d Exact Geometry Render Prompt

Use case: historical-scene
Asset type: Graphics V2 geometry-match experiment render, not final styled art.

Input images:
- Image 1: hard low 3/4 geometry target. Treat this as the composition authority.
- Image 2: north-up overhead map/control. Use only to confirm the three-building farmstead and west peat bog placement.
- Image 3: original historic map crop. Use only as source-linework veto authority.

Primary request:
Render a sparse low 3/4 game background geometry plate from Image 1 while preserving the exact farmstead geometry from the map.

Critical geometry constraints:
- Keep exactly three small farm buildings in the same relative positions as Image 1.
- Keep their shapes small, irregular, and clustered. Do not regularize them into a neat courtyard, village, scenic compound, or row of house rectangles.
- Preserve the west/left textured area as peat bog or bog-edge ground.
- Preserve the faint diagonal source-map linework as pale background context only. It is not a road, path, driveway, lane, wall, fence, or boundary to render as a physical feature.
- Do not add any new buildings, roads, lanes, paths, yards, loops, gardens, carts, people, animals, smoke, fences, or decorative foreground features.
- Do not transform the orange control box into a wall, yard, path, or object.
- Remove labels, printed letters, numerals, and green control marker colors from the final render.

Camera and render:
- Low 3/4 oblique game background camera, matching Image 1's pitch and footprint.
- Orthographic or near-orthographic feel; no fisheye, no wide-angle lens, no shrinking distant buildings.
- This is a geometry blockout render, not a beauty pass. It may be sparse and map-like if needed to preserve the source geometry.

Materials:
- Muted historic-map parchment ground with light watercolor texture.
- Three buildings may be simple roof-and-wall masses with tiny readable door faces, but their footprints and positions matter more than polish.
- Bog reads as soft muted olive-gray peat texture, not water.

Avoid:
- Picturesque crossroads, Y-shaped roads, formal farmyards, continuous wall grids, hedge mazes, gardens, extra cottages, road exits, scenic props, strong stone walls, modern objects, text, labels, marker dots, dark void doors.
