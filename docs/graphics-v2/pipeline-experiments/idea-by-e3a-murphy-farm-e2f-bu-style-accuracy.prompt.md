# Idea BY E3a — Murphy's Farm E2f BU-Style Accuracy Prompt

Use case: style-transfer / historical-scene
Asset type: Murphy's Farm Graphics V2 no-UI background plate, style-last pass.

Intent:
Apply the established BU E2 concept-realism / parish-notebook art style to the
accepted Murphy E2f geometry candidate while preserving map accuracy.

Input roles:

- Image 1: E2f Murphy's Farm rendered geometry. Edit target and highest
  authority for composition, camera, west peat bog, source-linework placement,
  and the compact three-building farmstead.
- Image 2: E2d Murphy's Farm geometry target. Hard veto authority for building
  count, farmstead footprint, and relative positions.
- Image 3: Murphy's Farm source map crop. Veto authority against extra roads,
  yards, landmarks, water, walls, gardens, labels, or buildings.
- Image 4: BU E2 concept-realism target. Style/material reference only.
- Door-fixed style crops: use only for fitted wooden plank doors, thresholds,
  stained limewash, thatch/slate texture, rough ink, and hand-painted detail.
- Original parish notebook image, if visible: style mood only. Do not copy its
  UI, people, signs, shop, church, graveyard, bridge, animals, crossroads, or
  road layout.

Primary request:
Convert E2f into a finished illustrated no-UI background plate in the BU E2
style family. Preserve the E2f/E2d layout more strongly than the style target.

Accuracy requirements:

- Exactly three small farm buildings, no more and no fewer.
- Preserve the same tight cluster and relative positions as E2f/E2d.
- Keep the west/left peat bog or bog-edge terrain as dark rough wet turf,
  muted heather/grass, and subtle peat texture. Do not turn it into water.
- Preserve the broad field-line relationships as faint environmental evidence
  only. Do not make the diagonal source-map linework into a road, lane,
  driveway, wall, fence, hedge, garden edge, or path.
- Do not add a yard enclosure, formal courtyard, garden, road loop, road exit,
  scenic crossroads, boundary wall grid, extra cottage, shed, cart, person,
  animal, smoke, sign, label, UI, church, shop, graveyard, bridge, or river.
- The E2d/E2f control-box-like line around the farmstead is nonphysical; do not
  render it as a fence, wall, path, or yard border.

Style requirements:

- Warm hand-inked watercolor parish notebook realism, no UI.
- Rough brown-black ink, paper grain, soft watercolor staining, dirty limewash,
  moss, weeds, ochre mud, sparse practical wear, and handmade irregularity.
- Keep the plate readable in neutral daylight, not over-dark.
- Small farm buildings may have rough thatch or slate, but no chimneys, roof
  nubs, roof posts, ridge stacks, or decorative vents.
- Every visible person-sized opening on a walkable facade must contain a fitted
  wooden plank door with a threshold/step. No black doorway voids.
- County Roscommon boundary prior: where any edge must be suggested, prefer
  soft hedgerow, bank, ditch, remnant hedge, or stone-earthen bank. Do not add
  continuous stone walls; if a tiny stone patch appears, it must be irregular
  mortarless fieldstone, not uniform rectangular blocks or bead chains.

Success:
The render should read as Murphy's Farm in the accepted BU E2 / concept-art
style while still passing the E2f geometry audit: three buildings, west bog,
source linework not promoted, no invented roads/yards/buildings.
