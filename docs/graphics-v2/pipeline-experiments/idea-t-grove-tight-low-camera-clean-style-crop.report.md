# Idea T: Grove Tight Low-Camera Clean Style Crop

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool and inspected visually from the saved PNG.

## Topology Preservation vs Cycle M/R

The plate preserves a coherent Grove-local subset rather than the whole Cycle M/R site: enclosed farmyard, long primary building, one attached/nearby outbuilding, one detached outbuilding, stone walls, gates, a garden edge behind the wall, and road/yard exits cropped off-frame. The building thresholds connect plausibly to the yard, and the road/wall relationships read as a continuous farm cluster. Fidelity is weaker than Cycle M as a full topology artifact because the crop omits much of the surrounding road network, the eastern tall barn relationship is simplified, and exact footprint positions are partially reinterpreted.

## Camera/Scale vs Cycle R/S and Original Notebook

This is clearly tighter and more human-scale than Cycle R/S. Doors, wall faces, thresholds, ruts, weeds, and stone thickness are large enough to stage sprites against, and the frame mostly avoids empty fields. The camera is lower than R/S and closer to the notebook building crop, with visible facades and side walls. It still sits a little higher than the original notebook scene/style crop: roof planes remain prominent, and some roof tiling reads more survey-clean than hand-notebook close-up.

## Effect of Cleaned Low-Camera Style Crop

Image 5 appears to have helped: the walls have stained limewash, the yard is muddier and more scumbled, vegetation hugs the buildings, and door/facade scale is more legible. The cleaned crop also avoided the worst broad-scene semantic leaks from the full notebook reference. Compared with Cycle S, this has stronger local tactility and less full-location coverage pressure.

## Semantic/Layout Leaks

No visible UI, text, signs, people, animals, carts, water, bridges, churches, graveyards, shops, smoke, or chimneys. The main leakage risk is layout-level: the model invented/normalized a very tidy enclosed compound with two visible gates and simplified building relationships. Garden rows behind the wall are still somewhat regular, though less dominant than in R/S.

## Problems/Risks

Roof hatching is improved but still too grid-like in places. Stone walls are chunky and readable, but some courses repeat as bead-like chains. The yard is useful and muddy, yet the whole scene is compositionally neat enough to retain some generated-game cleanliness. Because the crop is so tight, it is better for playable staging than for proving Grove-wide topology.

## Better Than Cycle R/S?

Yes for the stated Cycle T goal: it is a better close playable background plate, with lower camera, larger building details, stronger facade readability, and fewer survey-plate traits. Cycle R/S remain better as broader topology references, but this is the stronger direction for sprite-scale Grove background production.
