Modifications made
------------------
The raw raster tiles have been converted to an illustrated parchment art
style using the parish-tile-art pipeline (see parish/crates/parish-tile-art/).
Modifications include:
  - Per-pixel colour remap to a warm sepia/parchment palette
  - Sobel edge detection and ink-stroke overlay
  - Deterministic Perlin paper-grain texture
  - Optional AI img2img stylization via local Stable Diffusion

These modifications produce derivative tiles intended for use as in-game
map artwork in the Rundale interactive fiction game.  The derivative tiles
are distributed under the same CC-BY licence as the original work.

Pre-release coordination
------------------------
Before any public release that includes pre-generated (bundled) stylized
tiles, coordinate with the National Library of Scotland at geo@nls.uk to
confirm their preferred distribution arrangement for stylized derivatives.
See docs/design/map-evolution.md for background.
