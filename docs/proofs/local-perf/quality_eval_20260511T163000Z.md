## Observations

1. **Sprinkle-only clause is high-leverage.** A 13-line addition to the
   directive recovered 0.36 Overall for the 7B model. Cheaper than a
   model swap.

2. **1.5B got slightly worse**, falling 0.24 to 2.96. Its already-flat
   voice didn't gain from the new instructions and may have lost
   slack. Still acceptable for Intent/Reaction/Sim; never for Dialogue.

3. **14B kept its lead, but barely.** 0.36 Overall is at the edge of
   the 0.3 noise threshold. With 7B's failure mode patched, the
   decision to tier-up to 14B is now mostly about whether the host has
   memory headroom, not whether 7B will embarrass itself.

4. **Code-switch quality is still uneven.** Y prompt #1: "Tá mo chuid
   lánaí eile" reads as grammatically odd Irish ("my share of other
   children" literally). The model knows where to put a sprinkle but
   not always what to put there. A future improvement: pre-approved
   ga-IE word list pasted into the prompt as examples.

## Recommendation

- **8 GB Mac:** 1.5B everywhere. No Dialogue split. Tolerable.
- **16 GB Mac:** 1.5B small + **7B Dialogue.** Now safe — the
  code-switch catastrophe is patched.
- **24 GB+ Mac:** 1.5B small + **14B Dialogue.** Cleaner, but the
  marginal gain over fixed-7B is small (0.36 Overall).
