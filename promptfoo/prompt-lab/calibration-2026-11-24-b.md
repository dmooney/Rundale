# Calibration sheet 2: a draft improv prompt on flash-lite (2026-11-24)

Twenty replies from a draft improv-style prompt (`prompts/improv-draft.yaml`,
written to design §3.1 after you marked every reply on the first sheet "kills
it") on the production model, `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`, through OpenRouter. The
scenes are the same twenty as on the first sheet, in a shuffled order. Each
reply was sampled once. The draft is not a lab round and is not proposed for
production.

For each reply, mark whether it **keeps the scene going** or **kills it**: after
this line, would you want to say something, and would you know what to say?
Please also mark any line where the character makes up something that is not in
their world (a person, a place, a past event); a word on why helps tune the
rubric. The judges' scores are left out on purpose, so they do not sway your
reading; they are compared with your marks afterwards.

The replies are unedited. Everything a character says should come from their
world: Kilteevan (the village road, the Letter Office, Connolly Cottage), the
three villagers, and what anyone in Co. Roscommon would know in spring 1820.
